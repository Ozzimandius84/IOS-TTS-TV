The bar, on its own

Scroll this column and the bar goes, the way the traffic lights go in full screen. Bring the cursor near the top edge and it comes back. Press ⌘F, or ⌘K, and it comes back with the caret in it. Drag a file over the window and it comes back to catch it.

It is the window's width exactly, and the first 72 points are left for the buttons — in the app the real ones show through that gap rather than being drawn.

Nothing here scales with the window. The book does; the chrome does not.
